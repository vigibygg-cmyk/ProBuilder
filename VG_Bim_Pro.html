For photos/images, scale is always set manually: mark two points whose real distance you know (e.g. door width) and enter it in millimeters.
Click 📏 Calibrate and mark two points whose real distance you know on the drawing (e.g. overall dimension). Zoom in before marking for higher precision – the accuracy of all measurements depends on this.
–
Click 📐 Measure, then click two points on the drawing – the distance will be shown (live distance is displayed while dragging the second point, also in magnifier).

To adjust an existing point – hold and drag it to a new location. To move the measurement label sideways (to avoid covering the drawing) – hold and drag the label itself.
Click 📊 Layout, then mark segment start A and end B (e.g. wall corners). Specify the interval step (e.g. 600 mm) – the system will distribute points along the segment.

Dragging an interior point in table or drawing (or typing a value) adjusts only that spacing – A and B remain locked, and remaining points downstream re-distribute automatically using the interval, inserting an extra point if needed so total A–B distance stays exact.

You can drag A or B if marked inaccurately – the entire segment layout will recalculate for the new total distance.

Drag measurement labels sideways to keep drawings clearly visible – point positions remain fixed.
